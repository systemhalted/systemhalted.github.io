How to play

  1. Put every prop from the setup list on a table.
  2. One couple stands on either side of the table, hands behind their backs.
  3. The host plays a song. The first partner to grab the prop the song names wins the round.
  4. A wrong grab does not count; the other partner can still win.
  5. Every couple plays at least twice and switches sides between turns.

Keys

  • P play or stop the song
  • Space reveal the prop
  • ← / → left or right player wins
  • 0 nobody wins
  • Enter next round · Backspace previous round
  • F fullscreen · ? this help
