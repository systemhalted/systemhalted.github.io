A note on leadership, management, and the long road

Kartavya Path means "the path of duty" — short essays on leading teams, doing the harder right thing over the easier wrong one, and the long arc of building anything that lasts. These essays now publish on the blog, and selected ones are cross-posted to the LinkedIn newsletter — subscribe there, or follow the blog by RSS.
