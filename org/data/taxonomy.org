#+TITLE: Site taxonomy

* Series
Multi-part series, meant to be read in order.
** Series 1 - Language and Linguistics
A six-part series on how language works: grammar, vocabulary, pronunciation, script, and non-verbal communication.

** Series 2 - Turtle, BASIC, and the Long Road to Taste
How early languages — Logo, BASIC, GOTO-era code — shaped programming taste.

** Series 3 - Project Jigsaw (JPMS)
The Java module system, one concept at a time.

** Series 4 - Floating Point Without Tears
IEEE 754 doubles, BigDecimal, NaN, and how to compute with them without being surprised.

* Tech, Software, and Engineering
Software, programming, tools, and engineering practice.
** Technology
Tools, platforms, and the tech industry: hardware, operating systems, terminals and editors, services, and how they hold up in daily use.

** Software Engineering
The practice of building software: languages, design, testing, process, and lessons from real projects.

** Computer Science
The concepts beneath the practice: language theory, data structures, distributed systems, compilers, and coursework notes.

* AI, Data, and Digital Risk
Artificial intelligence, machine learning, data, and online safety.
** AI
Artificial intelligence and machine learning: what the systems can do, where they fail, and what they change about work.

** Data
Data protection, privacy, and how personal data is collected and used.

* Politics, Economy, and Society
Democracy, policy, corruption, economy, and social fabric.
** Politics & Governance
Democracy, elections, policy, law, and the conduct of the state, mostly in India.

** Society & Economy
Social and economic life: migration, literacy, inequality, and how communities change.

* Books, Reviews, and Media
Book reviews, media reviews, and other curated content.
** Books & Reading
Book reviews and notes on reading, across fiction, history, and periodicals.

** Movie Review
Reviews of films.

** Media Review
Reviews of shows, apps, and other media that are not books or films.

** Article Review
Responses to essays and articles written by others.

* Language, Literature, and Spirituality
Poetry, languages, philosophy, religion, and related writing.
** Languages & Linguistics
How languages work: grammar, script, usage, and communication.

** Poetry & Literature
Poems, mostly in Hindi and Urdu, and writing about literature.

** Spirituality & Philosophy
Philosophy and religion: Vedanta, free will, and questions of belief.

* Life, Work, and Personal Growth
Personal essays, behaviour, growth, and people-focused writing.
** Personal Essays
First-person writing on life, work, memory, and opinion.

** Life Events
Milestones, years in review, and remembering people who mattered.

* Hobbies, Travel, and Exploration
Astronomy, sports, travel, and other hobbies.
** Travel
Trips and places, written as they happened.

** Sports & Hobbies
Cricket, football, cycling, astronomy, and other things done for their own sake.

* Meta and Formats
Guest posts, archived speeches, and content tied to talks or videos.
** Meta & Guest
Guest posts and archived speeches, talks, and videos by others.
