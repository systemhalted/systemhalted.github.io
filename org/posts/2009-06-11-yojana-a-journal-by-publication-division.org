There are several books and journals that are published by[[http://publicationsdivision.nic.in/][Publication Division]], [[http://mib.nic.in/][Ministry of Information and Broadcasting]], [[http://goidirectory.nic.in/][GOI]]. One of the journal is [[http://www.yojana.gov.in/][Yojana]]. Yojana [[http://www.yojana.gov.in/about.asp][is]] a monthly devoted to socio-economic issues and started its publication in 1957 with Mr. Khuswant Singh as its Chief Editor. The magazine is now published in 13 languages viz. English, [[http://yojana.gov.in/hindi/default.asp][Hindi]], [[http://yojana.gov.in/hindi/regional/urdu.htm][Urdu]], [[http://yojana.gov.in/hindi/regional/punjabi.htm][Punjabi]], [[http://yojana.gov.in/hindi/regional/marathi.htm][Marathi]], [[http://www.yojana.gov.in/regional/gujarati.htm][Gujarati]], [[http://www.yojana.gov.in/regional/bengali.htm][Bengali]], [[http://www.yojana.gov.in/regional/assamese.htm][Assamese]], [[http://www.yojana.gov.in/regional/telugu.htm][Telugu]], [[http://www.yojana.gov.in/regional/tamil.htm][Tamil]], [[http://www.yojana.gov.in/regional/kannada.htm][Kannada]], [[http://www.yojana.gov.in/regional/malayalam.htm][Malayalam]]and [[http://www.yojana.gov.in/regional/oriya.htm][Oriya]].
