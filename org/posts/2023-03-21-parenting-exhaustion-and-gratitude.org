#+TITLE: I Sat Down to Write About Parenting Exhaustion
#+DESCRIPTION: What began as an attempt to write about the exhaustion of parenting became a reflection on my children’s love and the gratitude I feel as their father.

#+CATEGORIES: Personal Essays
#+TAGS: parenting, fatherhood, family, love, gratitude
#+COMMENTS: true
#+TOC: false

I sat down to write about how exhausting parenting can be. But as I began, I found myself thinking about the love and affection my children give me, and what I wanted to say changed.

The exhaustion is real. There are days when I feel overwhelmed, lose patience, or simply want some time to myself. Yet I can be tired and frustrated and still feel deeply grateful to be their father. Those feelings often exist together.

Their affection means so much to me. It does not make every difficult day easy, but it reminds me how much I value being part of their lives and having them in mine.

I want to remember that alongside the responsibilities and the moments that test my patience. I want to appreciate our time together while we are living it, even when I am too tired to do that as well as I would like.

I started writing because I needed to express my exhaustion. I ended up wanting to say how much I love my children.
