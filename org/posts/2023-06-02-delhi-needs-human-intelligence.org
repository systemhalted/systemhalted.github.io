#+TITLE: Delhi Needs Some Human Intelligence
#+DESCRIPTION: A little road sense and consideration for fellow citizens would go a long way towards improving Delhi’s traffic.
#+DATE: 2023-06-02
#+CATEGORIES: Personal Essays
#+TAGS: Delhi, traffic, civic sense, artificial intelligence
#+COMMENTS: true
#+TOC: false

Delhi doesn’t need artificial intelligence[fn:1]. A little human intelligence about how to drive on the road, and a little emotional intelligence to show compassion towards fellow citizens, would be sufficient.

Well, perhaps "sufficient" is asking too much. But following traffic rules, maintaining lane discipline, and allowing others to pass would be a good start.

An intelligent traffic management system could help. I just wish we brought a little more intelligence and consideration to the driving ourselves.
