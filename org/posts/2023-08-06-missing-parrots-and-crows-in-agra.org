#+TITLE: Where Were the Parrots and Crows?
#+DESCRIPTION: After visiting Agra, I realized what had felt missing: I had not seen any parrots or crows.
#+DATE: 2023-08-06
#+CATEGORIES: Personal Essays
#+TAGS: Agra, India, birds, observations
#+COMMENTS: true
#+TOC: false

During my recent visit to Agra, I sensed that something was missing, but I could not figure out what.

Just now, it struck me: I did not see any parrots or crows.

Peacocks, yes. Pigeons, certainly. But no parrots or crows.

Perhaps they were around and I simply missed them. Still, it is strange how something so familiar can escape your attention until you realize you have not seen it.
