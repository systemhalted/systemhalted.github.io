"Let’s start fresh" is my new motto.

After a recent setback, I found myself feeling disappointed, disheartened, and disillusioned. It was particularly difficult because this was not the first time. When something happens repeatedly, it becomes harder to treat it as an isolated disappointment. I began asking why it keeps happening and whether the steps I have taken to overcome previous setbacks have changed anything.

It was easy to stay unhappy about things not going as planned. But I needed to think about what I could do differently.

"एक घुमाव लाने की ज़रूरत है।"

A change in direction is needed.

Starting fresh means taking an honest look at what happened, carrying forward what I have learned, and being willing to change my approach. Persistence matters, but so does recognizing when persistence alone is not enough.

Am I still hungry? Yes, absolutely.

I still want to grow, take on meaningful challenges, and see what I am capable of achieving. This disappointment has not taken that away. It has made me pause and reconsider how I move forward.

I cannot say I am grateful for every setback while I am going through it. Some are painful, and it takes time to understand what to learn from them. But I can take responsibility for my next steps: seek honest feedback, question my assumptions, and act on what I discover.

I do not have everything figured out yet. I am ready to begin again.
