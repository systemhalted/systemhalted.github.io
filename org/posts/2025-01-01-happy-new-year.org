Positive. Peace. Powerful.

As I look forward to the new year, I’ve chosen to anchor my journey on these three words. They represent the mindset and energy I aim to cultivate in everything I do--professionally and personally.

Positive: To approach every challenge with optimism, to see opportunities where others see obstacles, and to uplift those around me.

Peace: To find balance amidst chaos, create harmony in my relationships, and foster an environment of calm and understanding.

Powerful: To take decisive actions, embrace growth, and empower myself and others to make a meaningful impact.

These words are my compass, guiding me to show up as the best version of myself in 2025. What three words would define your focus for the year ahead?
